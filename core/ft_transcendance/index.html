Single-Page App in TypeScript · Local 2-player · Deterministic paddle speed · Dockerized with HTTPS.

How to play

  • Two players on the same keyboard.
  • Left paddle: W / S · Right paddle: ↑ / ↓
  • First to 5 points wins the match.
